## Exact result

Fix a prime p, an elementary abelian group G=(Z/p)^m, and nonzero ordered
branch elements that span G and sum to zero. Let every branch point vary in
the full ordered configuration family. On a very general fiber, every rational
Hodge class on every power of the covering curve's Jacobian is algebraic.
The exceptional set is a single countable union of proper closed algebraic
subsets for all powers and degrees. For p=2 the classes are generated by
divisors. The odd-prime argument includes constant support-three factors,
repeated cyclic quotients, all Galois embeddings, and all effective determinant
words in each finite degree.

Structural companion Section 7 now extends this result to arbitrary finite
abelian groups of exponent dividing p^e (p odd), 3^a5^b or 3^a7^b. It also
gives a criterion for any odd exponent N conditional on all-powers HC for
the degree-N Fermat Jacobian. Its primitive-factor and rational assembly
proofs treat the conductor-15 constant factor explicitly. The table below
continues to describe the prime-exponent manuscript itself.

These are not results for arbitrary subfamilies or every special fiber.
They do not establish arbitrary even composite exponent, unrestricted odd
exponent without the Fermat premise, nonabelian covers, integral Hodge
classes, or the separate PEL/Weil-eightfold deformation problem.

## Where the formerly missing arguments are proved

|---|---|---|
| No higher-rank all-definite rational exception | Section 2, mixed-embedding lemma | Normalize an endpoint age to 1; each forward age increment is at most 1, so a mixed row must occur. An initially interior row needs no normalization. |
| Full projection at every embedding | Section 2, projection proposition | Match MN case (a) at a mixed row, normalize affine configurations with finite scalar fiber monodromy, and transport the K-defined closure through Galois conjugation. |
| Support separation including zero pair sums | Section 3 | MN's spanning-vector relation and rank-one formula make every active pair twist projectively nonidentity, including its unipotent case. A twist disappears after an inactive point is forgotten. |
| Exact connected blocks | Section 3 | Normalizers compare original braid images before finite base change. Higher rank gives one global sign; four labelled points give precisely the sign cube ±V4. Goursat then supplies the product. |
| Actual Hodge orientations | Section 3 | Explicit deck-equivariant Möbius/Kummer graphs identify (V4.c)∩C. The negative component is treated as the polarized dual. No invariance of C under V4 is assumed. |
| Rational determinant source | Section 4 | Rational deck averages match all raw slots, then alternate. The faithful K action comes from one slot, even when p divides the determinant rank. |
| All invariant diagrams realized algebraically | Section 5 | Schur–Weyl gives positive volume and coevaluation diagrams. Actual graph maps, rational polarization tensors and global matching projectors realize each complete Galois orbit. Empty words require all deck translates, not only a trace. |
| Relations/cancellations and Hodge descent | Section 6 | The finite rational algebraic source map surjects onto monodromy invariants. A polarization complement lifts Hodge classes. The splitting need not be algebraic; only the forward map transfers cycles. |
| Balanced sources are the only Hodge contributors | Section 4 and Section 6 | The rational Hodge subspace of a K-line is K-stable, hence zero or the whole line. Nonzero means every embedding has middle bidegree, exactly the fusion balance condition. |
| Algebraic cycles | Fermat companion, Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 | Determinants are algebraic direct summands of prime-degree Fermat cohomology; Shioda supplies Hodge classes on products. Algebraic diagram maps transfer them to the Jacobian power. Fusion is an alternative. |
| Binary all powers | Section 7 | A'Campo full symplectic projections, independent supports, a nonseparating vanishing-cycle argument and symplectic tensor invariants give divisor generation. |

No effective monoid is replaced by an integer lattice basis. A finite spanning
list is used separately in each degree, so negative multiplicities never enter
the cycle construction. A pure Hodge splitting is used only to establish
existence of a source Hodge lift; no algebraic splitting is assumed.

## False shortcuts explicitly excluded

- At p=5 the word (1,1,1,2) has definite conjugate embeddings. Opposite
  characters are isomorphic as SL2 modules but need not have a nonzero
  degree-zero Hodge Hom. Thus the full monodromy commutant is not being
  identified with the Hodge endomorphism algebra.
- At p=3, (1,2,2,2,2) has zero sum on every pair involving its first label.
  Support separation cannot assume a semisimple pair always exists.
- At p=3, twelve copies of 1 have ages 4 and 8, with no age-2 embedding.
  The lemma proves existence of a mixed embedding, not age 2 in every word.
- Diagonal deck action on all h determinant slots can be trivial when p|h.
  The argument uses the faithful action induced from one raw slot.
- The trace of a Galois orbit of pairing diagrams alone need not span it.
  The empty-word case uses a full invertible cyclotomic Vandermonde matrix.

## Reproducible checks

`scripts/check_general.py` is standard-library-only and writes deterministic
JSON. Its fixed ranges are:

- 13,268 normalized partitions for odd primes through 31, with exact carry
  identities and mixed embeddings. These cover all endpoint-normalizable
  candidate exceptions at those primes, not all arbitrary branch words.
- All 69,644 labelled nonzero four-words at odd primes through 31, forming
  9,904 projective pair-fingerprint fibers, each exactly its ±V4 orbit.
- 2,166 higher-rank words: support five at p=3,5,7 and support six at p=3,5.
- Exact empty-word Vandermonde arithmetic modulo the cyclotomic polynomial
  at p=2 and every odd prime through 31; no floating-point roots.
- The three explicit false-shortcut examples above, binary signature cases,
  and rejection of a malformed nonzero-sum word.

Normal and optimized Python produce identical reports; checks use an explicit
helper that cannot be disabled by optimization. The combined replay runner
includes this script, the previous repair checks, the byte-identical Phase II
replay, eight frozen-source hashes and all five review-PDF/source hash pairs.
The additional structural checker covers 152 odd-subgroup cases through
p=103, exact signature stabilizers and first-exceptional dimensions,
7,746 volume-word balance checks at p=7,13,19, Koszul signs through rank six,
and evaluation hyperplanes through rank eight. Its odd-exponent checks cover
11 primitive projectors, six character inventories and 24,159 primitive
support-four words at odd conductors up to 45, with the conductor-15 CM type.
No enumeration proves the universal monodromy, Hodge, or cycle statement.

## Review and remaining work

Three internal agent reviews separately checked the geometric classification,
rational generator construction and their assembly. They found and corrected
an affine-versus-moduli fundamental-group normalization, the actual-code orbit
intersection, a determinant-line choice, and source convention/locator details.
Agreement among agents is not mathematical certification; the proof text and
its imported theorems are the evidence to inspect.

The general all-powers manuscript no longer has an unproved generator
hypothesis. The Fermat companion supplies the principal algebraicity route;
the structural companion now supplies the exact Hodge-group and determinant
quotient formulas. These are written arguments, not full Lean proofs.

The [novelty audit](NOVELTY.md) materially narrows the contribution: the
mixed-determinant algebraicity is classical, and the unrestricted cyclic
all-powers case follows from Rohde's 2007 theorem and old cycle inputs.
The remaining candidate contribution is the uniform cross-character group
classification and its assembly. Independent specialist review and exact
priority assessment remain necessary; Duke/JEMS level is not supported by
the present evidence.
