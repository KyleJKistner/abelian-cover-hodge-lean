## The revised theorem

For every odd prime p and a very general complex parameter t outside
{0,1,infinity}, take the smooth projective normalization of

    u^p = x/(x-t),    v^p = x-1.

Writing F = Q(zeta_p + zeta_p^-1), the revised proof gives:

- Genus (p-1)^2.
- A decomposition into pairwise non-isogenous absolutely simple factors of
  dimension (p-1)/2, each with endomorphism field F; two factors occur
  with multiplicity two and (p-3)/2 factors with multiplicity four.
- Full generic endomorphism algebra M_2(F)^2 × M_4(F)^((p-3)/2).
- Derived Hodge group (Res_(F/Q) SL_2)^((p+1)/2). The rational
  symplectic-centralizer calculation also identifies the full Hodge group
  with this semisimple group.
- Néron–Severi rank (p-1)(5p-9)/2.
- Rational Hodge classes generated by divisors on every power of this very
  general Jacobian.

These are mathematical manuscript proofs relative to named classical and
Menet–Nguyen inputs. The finite script checks supporting identities;
it is not an infinite computation or a geometric formalization.
The statement is not about every special fibre or every finite-abelian cover.

## Replacement for split rank-two independence

1. The cyclic quotient for (a,b) has **three finite labels** (a,b,p-a)
   at (0,1,t), with infinity residue -b. At a unit row k the finite
   fractional sum is 1 + {kb/p}, strictly between one and two.
2. Here p≥3, all labels lie in 1,...,p-1, the effective quotient is
   connected, and the root is the source's exp(-2*pi*i*k/p).
   Menet–Nguyen Theorem 2.1 gives rank two and signature (1,1);
   Definition 1.1(a)/Theorem 5.1 gives full SU(1,1) projection.
   Affine-coordinate monodromy is finite scalar, hence projectively
   trivial: the full projective representation descends to M_0,4.
3. Theorem 2.5 and the reflection calculation immediately preceding
   Corollary 2.7 give, for the **same** braids (12) and (23), unordered
   eigenvalue ratios zeta_p^(±(a+b)) and zeta_p^(±(b-a)).
   Corollary 2.7 supplies the unipotent case; no nonidentity of that
   unipotent is needed.
4. A connected graph is normal in the full two-factor closure.
   Its normalizer in PSL_2 × PSL_2 is the graph itself. Thus connected
   graph relations force the full braid images to have matching
   projective conjugacy invariants.
5. Independent signs give the following equations, solved using p odd:

       a'+b' = eps(a+b),    a'-b' = eta(a-b),
       a' = ((eps+eta)/2)a + ((eps-eta)/2)b,
       b' = ((eps-eta)/2)a + ((eps+eta)/2)b.

   Therefore the pair is one of ±(a,b), ±(b,a), including the slopes
   a=b and a=-b.
6. The actual involutions Phi, Psi, Omega realize those isomorphisms.
   Semisimple Goursat assembly gives exactly those independent blocks.

This removes the restricted theorem's dependence on the missing

## Corrections that matter mathematically

### Galois-saturated moving part

The original lemma at phase_I_complete.tex:193 incorrectly removed each
definite complex eigenspace. An individual eigenspace need not preserve an
integral lattice.

The revision's Proposition “Galois-saturated moving criterion” uses
**rational cyclotomic character orbits**. If any embedding is positive,
full projection there annihilates its fixed vectors; deck-field linearity
annihilates the fixed subspace at every embedding. The entire orbit is
moving, including its definite embeddings.

If every embedding is definite, the whole rational summand has an
integral lattice and compact real monodromy, hence finite image. This is
the valid use of compactness plus integrality.

The criterion exposes its full-projection premise and matches it to
Menet–Nguyen for effective order at least three: put one active label at
infinity, so n=s-1 and the finite sum is Q(c)-alpha_infinity in (1,n-1).
Order two uses the separate A'Campo input. The criterion does not assert
a complete graph classification between the resulting rational factors.

Diagnostic: (1,1,1,2)/5 has signatures, up to orientation,
(2,0),(1,1),(1,1),(0,2). Its definite embeddings cannot be discarded
from the rational orbit containing the noncompact ones.

### Oriented quotient-graph maps

The original assertions at lines 487–490 confused a cyclic-curve
isomorphism with a specified eigenspace arrow. With a fixed primitive
root on both curves, v=h*y maps the chosen eigenspace to the chosen
eigenspace, whereas v=h/y maps it to the opposite eigenspace.
Genuine oriented arrows are indexed by e=Pc. The relation e=±Pc alone
does not supply every specified Hodge Hom corner.

Matrix saturation is now proved only on components connected by these
actual oriented arrows. A missing internal standard–dual map remains
missing after transport across a known graph; the former blanket
cross-Hom assertion cannot bypass that issue.

### Inverse graphs replace the Rosati shortcut

The original matrix-unit proof also treated a Rosati adjoint as the
inverse of an oriented arrow. For the cyclotomic-linear Rosati extension,
e_c^dagger=e_-c, so the adjoint of W_c→W_e has source W_-e.
It need not compose with the original arrow as claimed.

The revision uses algebraic inverse graphs and q_* q^* = (deg q) id.
For inverse pairs T_i:W_0→W_i and S_i:W_i→W_0, the operators
E_ij=T_i S_j are exact matrix units. No rescaling by a square root is
needed.

In the split family, Psi explicitly switches the character to its
negative. Thus both orientations really are connected. The crossed
product K ⋊ Gal(K/F)=M_2(F), together with the slope-swapping graph,
supplies a rational lower algebra; its dimension matches the full complex
monodromy commutant. No unspecified descent of one cyclotomic graph is
used.

## General scope and withdrawn assertions

This split-family paper proves the rational moving criterion and its specified
split family. The separate `general_all_powers.tex` now supplies the complete
prime-exponent connected-monodromy blocks and the general rational all-powers
argument. Its proof uses all Galois embeddings, actual oriented graphs and
balanced determinant sources; it does not need the missing Fricke/Fox core.
The structural companion now supplies the exact generic Hodge group and
determinant quotient in Section 2, and the odd-exponent extension with
unconditional Aoki cases in Section 7.

The following stronger assertions are still not supplied:

- the old real product formula deleting compact complex embeddings;
- equality of every monodromy-Hom corner with algebraic Hodge graph maps;
- the legacy blanket endomorphism formula outside the separately proved split case;
- arbitrary even composite exponent or unrestricted odd exponent without the
  Fermat-Jacobian premise.

The blanket cross-corner claim remains false. The general proof treats
negative orientations as polarized duals and retains volume tensors as
rank-one K Hodge structures until their all-row balance is proved. The
Fermat companion supplies their cycles, with fusion as an alternative.
