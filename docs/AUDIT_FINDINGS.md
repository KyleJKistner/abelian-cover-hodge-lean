# Mathematical audit findings

These findings were discovered while translating the supplied Phase I/II
package. They are disclosed here because suppressing them would make the Lean
boundary misleading. The provenance copies in `manuscripts/` remain unchanged.

## AF-1 — Kummer relation lattice is not saturated

**Location:** `phase_II_complete.tex`, the construction around lines 256–275;
ledger item P2.5, with downstream use in P2.7–P2.9.

The manuscript presents the character lattice of the connected determinant
torus as the raw determinant lattice modulo relations generated by
`ε_c - ε_d` and `ε_c + ε_d`. If a Kummer stabilizer identifies a rank-two
block `V_c` with `V_c^∨`, this prescription can impose only `2 ε_c = 0`.
The resulting quotient has `Z/2` torsion, whereas the character lattice of a
connected algebraic torus is torsion-free.

The candidate correction is to quotient by the saturation

\[
R_{\mathrm{Kum}}^{\mathrm{sat}}
= \operatorname{Sat}\langle\varepsilon_c-\varepsilon_d,
\varepsilon_c+\varepsilon_d\rangle.
\]

The ultimate signature image may be unchanged because its target is
torsion-free, but P2.5 is false as written and its downstream proofs require a
fresh check. No Lean theorem in this repository assumes the unsaturated claim.

## AF-2 — Internal rank-two character is misnormalized

**Location:** `phase_II_complete.tex`, the internal-disk argument around line
394; ledger item P2.9.

The manuscript defines `ε_c` as the determinant character of `V_c`. A
standard-to-dual rank-two invariant transforms by `det(V_c)^{±1}`, hence has
character `±ε_c`, not `2ε_c`. The zero-signature conclusion may survive after
normalization, but the displayed character calculation and the accompanying
torsion-free argument do not match their own definition.

The conditional Lean assembly deliberately does not encode this calculation.

## AF-3 — Legacy Phase I certificate is incomplete

`phase_I_certificate.py` imports
`phase1src/p_ary_mt_defect_certificate.py`, but that source file was not present
in the recovered package. The frozen report records the missing file's SHA-256
as

`dd58de8adfae482319ccbfab2164bee2b5393ce27f0dbbef1c055a5784a67fff`.

A hash does not reconstruct the source. Consequently, the reported
Fox–Gassner and 7,077,120-vector computations are provenance records, not
currently reproducible certificates. The Lean core independently replaces the
finite statements it actually claims.

## Review consequence

It remains inaccurate to describe the headline all-powers Hodge theorem as
Lean verified: the manuscript-specific deductions in `Bridge/Assembly.lean`
are still abstract inputs.

However, the shortest all-powers route does not need the determinant quotient
or internal-disk criterion. Derived invariant theory followed by the direct
Hodge-bidegree test selects zero-signature determinant words, and the
Aoki/fusion/Schoen route algebraizes them. AF-1 and AF-2 therefore remain
blockers for the claimed complete Hodge group and endomorphism refinement, but
not necessary premises of the direct all-powers dependency scaffold.
