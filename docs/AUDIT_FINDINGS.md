# Mathematical audit findings

These findings were discovered while translating the supplied Phase I/II
package. They are disclosed here because suppressing them would make the Lean
boundary misleading. The provenance copies in `manuscripts/` remain unchanged.

## AF-1 — Kummer relation lattice is not saturated

**Location:** `phase_II_complete.tex`, the construction around lines 256–275;
ledger item P2.5, with downstream use in P2.7–P2.9.

The manuscript presents the character lattice of the connected determinant
torus as the raw determinant lattice modulo relations generated by
`ε_c - ε_d` and `ε_c + ε_d`. If a Kummer stabilizer identifies a rank-two
block `V_c` with `V_c^∨`, this prescription can impose only `2 ε_c = 0`.
The resulting quotient has `Z/2` torsion, whereas the character lattice of a
connected algebraic torus is torsion-free.

The correction is to quotient by the saturation

\[
R_{\mathrm{Kum}}^{\mathrm{sat}}
= \operatorname{Sat}\langle\varepsilon_c-\varepsilon_d,
\varepsilon_c+\varepsilon_d\rangle.
\]

`Mathlib/DeterminantLattice.lean` implements this saturation, proves that its
quotient is torsion-free, and proves that saturation does not change a
compatible signature image because the signature lattice is torsion-free.
`Mathlib/DeterminantSignature.lean` additionally proves this for concrete
standard/dual branch words up to coordinate permutation, including the
self-dual raw generator `2 ε_c`.
P2.5 is nevertheless false as written: identifying the corrected lattice with
the character lattice of the connected geometric torus still requires a fresh
proof. No Lean theorem assumes the unsaturated geometric claim.

## AF-2 — Internal rank-two character is misnormalized

**Location:** `phase_II_complete.tex`, the internal-disk argument around line
394; ledger item P2.9.

The manuscript defines `ε_c` as the determinant character of `V_c`. A
standard-to-dual rank-two invariant transforms by `det(V_c)^{±1}`, hence has
character `±ε_c`, not `2ε_c`. The zero-signature conclusion may survive after
normalization, but the displayed character calculation and the accompanying
torsion-free argument do not match their own definition.

The corrected polynomial identity `A J Aᵀ = det(A) J` and uniqueness of its
coefficient are now proved in `Mathlib/RankTwoDeterminant.lean`. Its geometric
interpretation as a Hodge-Hom criterion remains separate. See `TORUS_REPAIR.md`.

## AF-4 — The full-projection source must be Menet--Nguyen, not a factorwise reading of Spelta--Tamborini

**Location:** Phase I full-simple-projection step, ledger item I.3.

Spelta--Tamborini Theorem 4.4 assumes a condition on the whole cyclic family
(`no repeating factors`) and does not by itself supply the factorwise theorem
in the generality used by the manuscript. The viable individual-eigenspace
input is Menet--Nguyen Theorem 5.1, whose hypothesis is that the associated
sequence is `good` in their Definition 1.1. Consequently, the project must
prove the manuscript-specific hypothesis match

`positive Chevalley--Weil multiplicities -> good sequence`

before applying the source theorem. `Mathlib/MenetNguyenGood.lean` now proves
this arithmetic match on the nonzero support at a unit row. The separately
typed Theorem 5.1 input exposes degree, branch-count/range, connectedness,
primitive-root, and good-sequence premises; the Lean result does not assert
the monodromy conclusion. Menet--Nguyen remains an arXiv preprint.

## AF-5 — The prime balanced-tuple leaf has a narrower source domain

**Location:** Phase II balanced-to-simple step, ledger item P2.12.

Aoki's `B_m^n` setup requires nonzero entries, branch sum zero, unit-row
balance, and tuple length `n + 2` with `n` positive and even. Thus the prime
`B = D` statement recorded on p. 24 (and credited there to W. Parry) applies
to even tuples of length at least four. Omitting evenness also makes a naive
all-length statement false at `p = 2`: the one-entry tuple `[1]` satisfies the
doubled all-row equation but cannot be partitioned into opposite pairs.

`External.AokiPrimeBalanceInput` now exposes the full source domain and only
an existential pairing conclusion. The bridge proves odd-prime parity, branch
sum, and the restriction from all nonzero rows to unit rows. It proves the
length-zero and length-two pairings directly and invokes the source input only
at length at least four. The project theorem
`aokiBalanced_length_even_of_prime_ne_two` supplies the parity step for every
prime modulus other than two.

Aoki's one-page 1984 erratum has also been checked. It corrects only the
statement of Theorem B in the introduction; it does not affect the p. 24 prime
`B = D` statement used here or the surrounding Theorem A classification.

## AF-6 — Schoen's primitive-factor theorem has a narrower source scope

**Location:** Phase II simple-tuple cycle step, ledger item P2.15.

Schoen's Corollary 3.1 is in the section assuming `m > 2` and concerns the
primitive factor `B`, its Weil subspace, and the associated simple
local-monodromy tuple. The source input now exposes smooth irreducible complex
projectivity, faithful cyclic action, the quotient, associated-tuple and
primitive-rank identifications, `m > 2`, the primitive factor, and an explicit
opposite-pair witness. Identifying that factor with the whole prime cyclic
`P^1` Jacobian remains a separate manuscript obligation.

## AF-7 — Menet--Nguyen uses the opposite root convention, and extra source data are needed

**Location:** Phase I pair-spectrum and high-rank reconstruction steps, ledger
items I.5--I.7.

Menet--Nguyen Section 5 fixes
`q = exp(-2*pi*i*k/d)`, while Phase I defines the branch eigenvalues using a
positive exponential. For the same row, the source pair eigenvalue is the
inverse of the manuscript's displayed product. Applying the source formulas to
the opposite character may repair the argument, but invariance of the good
condition and identification of the two orientations are manuscript-specific
deductions and must be proved.

The pair-twist formula and Corollary 2.7 also do not by themselves supply every
claim made in Phase I. Nonidentity in the unipotent case uses the exact Gram
relations from Menet--Nguyen Theorem 2.2. The claimed prefix eigenspace and
complement use Lemma 3.9 and Corollary 3.11 in addition to Theorem 2.8.
`External/MenetNguyen.lean` now enforces negative-root/row alignment and
geometric operator identification, separates the nontrivial reflection
spectrum from literal Corollary 2.7, and records the root-sign and nonidentity
steps as project obligations. The remaining Theorem 2.2 and subset-eigenspace
interfaces are explicitly marked unmodeled.

## AF-8 — A compact complex eigenspace need not have integral monodromy

**Location:** legacy Phase I, lines 187–198, especially `positive-moving`.

The rational moving part cannot be obtained by discarding every definite
complex eigenspace. For `(1,1,1,2)/5`, the Galois embeddings have signatures
`(2,0),(1,1),(1,1),(0,2)`. Finiteness of the first image would force finiteness
of its noncompact conjugate. The individual embedding has no independent
integral lattice to justify that conclusion.

`manuscripts/phase_I_revised.tex` proves the corrected criterion on entire
rational cyclotomic orbits. A positive embedding and the matched full-projection
input annihilate fixed vectors on the entire orbit; an orbit all of whose
embeddings are definite has compact **rational integral** monodromy and finite
image. The split family has signature `(1,1)` at every active embedding and
does not require this general reduction.

## AF-9 — Sign-orbit equivalence does not supply every oriented graph arrow

**Location:** legacy Phase I, inverse-map formula around 487–490 and the
cross-corner argument at 527–558.

The quotient isomorphism with second coordinate `h/y` inverts the deck
generator, hence exchanges the chosen primitive eigenspace with the opposite
one. It must not be treated as a specified `W_c → W_e` arrow without checking
the root convention. Nor does transporting a missing internal standard–dual
map across a known graph make that map algebraic.

The revised Phase I manuscript indexes matrix units by actual oriented arrows
and withdraws the blanket general cross-Hom saturation assertion. In the split
family the explicit involution `Psi` supplies the needed opposite orientation.
The two-eigenratio proof independently determines its complete blocks.

## AF-10 — An oriented Rosati adjoint is not an inverse graph

**Location:** legacy Phase I, matrix-unit lemma around 513–525.

For the cyclotomic-linear extension of the bilinear Rosati involution,
`e_c† = e_-c`. An isolated arrow `W_c → W_e` therefore has adjoint on
`W_-e → W_-c`, not generally `W_e → W_c`. The displayed adjoint composite
cannot serve as its inverse on that oriented corner.

The revision uses algebraic inverse graphs and quotient normalization
`q_* q^* = (deg q) id`. For genuine inverse pairs `T_i,S_i`, the formula
`E_ij=T_i S_j` gives matrix units directly. The new Phase I certificate checks
both these products and the failure of the old oriented-adjoint shortcut.

## AF-11 — Determinant matching needs the scalar action from one tensor slot

**Location:** legacy Phase II determinant specialization and disconnected
pairing-graph assembly, roughly 600–649.

The rational target is a tensor product over the common cyclotomic field,
not the full rational tensor product with independent embeddings. Furthermore,
the exterior-power deck map on `det_K(V)` has eigenvalue `ζ^(b h)` when
`dim_K(V)=h`; the scalar `K`-action has eigenvalue `ζ^b`. The former can become
trivial when `p` divides `h`, so it cannot generally distinguish the embeddings.

`manuscripts/fusion_revised.tex` constructs the matching projector on the raw
`H^1` slots using `p^-1 Σ T_u^a T_v^-a`, then alternates and returns to the
Jacobian cohomology. Factorials and Koszul signs are explicit. The same algebraic
projector selects matching embeddings across disconnected pairing components.
The new finite checker includes repeated and disconnected factors and the case
`h=p`. Geometric algebraicity is supported by the written argument, not by
these finite checks alone.

## Review consequence

The current revisions and the status of every repair are linked in
[REPAIR_STATUS.md](REPAIR_STATUS.md). In particular, the Phase II certificate
has now been regenerated byte for byte in a pinned environment; the missing
Phase I core has not. The revised split-family proof avoids that core.


It remains inaccurate to describe the headline all-powers Hodge theorem as
Lean verified: the manuscript-specific deductions in `Bridge/Assembly.lean`
are still abstract inputs.

However, the shortest all-powers route does not need the determinant quotient
or internal-disk criterion. Derived invariant theory followed by the direct
Hodge-bidegree test selects zero-signature determinant words, and the
Aoki/fusion/Schoen route algebraizes them. AF-1 and AF-2 therefore remain
blockers for the claimed complete Hodge group and endomorphism refinement, but
not necessary premises of the direct all-powers dependency scaffold. This
does not establish that route: the general rational representation and
generator-exhaustion issues exposed by AF-8 through AF-10 remain unresolved.
The revised standalone fusion argument and its slot projectors address AF-11,
without assuming that they exhaust all Hodge generators.
